\begin{abstract}
\onehalfspacing
Deploying Small Language Models (SLMs, 0.5B to 4B parameters) on physical edge accelerators enables complete data sovereignty, bounded latency, and autonomous offline operation without cloud egress. However, operating under strict physical memory boundaries ($\le 4096\text{ MiB}$ VRAM) introduces acute trade-offs among tensor quantization compression, DRAM memory bus saturation, Time-To-First-Token (TTFT), and defensive safety alignment. This technical report presents a systems evaluation conducted on an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti GPU (4096\,MiB GDDR5, 128-bit bus, $112.0\text{ GB/s}$ theoretical bandwidth, Pascal sm\_61). We benchmark eight model configurations (\textbf{Qwen-2.5-0.5B}, \textbf{SmolLM2-1.7B}, \textbf{Gemma-4-E2B [MTP]}, \textbf{Gemma-4-E2B [IQ4\_XS]}, \textbf{Gemma-4-E2B [Q4\_K\_M]}, \textbf{Qwen-3.5-2B}, \textbf{Llama-3.2-3B}, and \textbf{Qwen-3.5-4B}) using the \texttt{llama-server} runtime (commit b4120+) with continuous asynchronous NVML hardware telemetry sampled at 250\,ms intervals. A $2 \times 2$ factorial ablation isolated performance drivers: weight quantization compression (IQ4\_XS vs Q4\_K\_M) accounted for $+28.2\%$ speedup ($34.51 \pm 0.29$ vs $26.92 \pm 0.23$\,tok/s, $t=65.41$), whereas speculative Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) under identical quantization yielded $+25.6\%$ causal speedup ($43.35 \pm 0.57$ vs $34.51 \pm 0.29$\,tok/s, $t=43.72$, $p < 10^{-15}$), resulting in an overall $+61.0\%$ compound configuration gain (Welch's $t = 84.50$, $p = 5.88 \times 10^{-18}$, Cohen's $d = 37.79$) with only 40\,MiB VRAM overhead for Q8\_0 draft heads. Systems feasibility modeling indicates that 4B architectures operate at the critical edge ($76.7\%$), requiring Q4\_0 KV cache compression for Gemma-3-4B to avert physical memory exhaustion ($4358\text{ MiB}$ required in uncompressed FP16). Defensive safety evaluations ($N=10$) demonstrated zero false positive refusals ($\text{FPR}=0.0$\%) on benign administrative tasks, but susceptibility to indirect instruction injection and delimiter breach (ASR between 50.0\% and 100.0\%). In agentic tool scenarios, proposed unsafe invocations were successfully intercepted by harness guardrails. Parallel corpus tokenization demonstrated a 1.35--2.33 tokens/word penalty for Portuguese compared to 1.10 for English. All raw traces, SHA-256 hashes, and code are open and reproducible.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Systems Context}

The expansion of autonomous applications across cyber-physical systems, mobile robotics, edge gateways, and microservers requires local natural language processing without continuous cloud connectivity~\cite{williams2009,leviathan2023}. Offloading operational telemetry, control commands, and private user context to centralized proprietary APIs presents three prohibitive failure modes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Data Sovereignty and Privacy:} Risk of confidential data transit through third-party servers and compliance constraints under data privacy regulations (GDPR/LGPD);
    \item \textbf{Temporal Determinism:} Variable WAN transit latencies ($100\text{ ms}$ to $2000\text{ ms}$) with acute vulnerability to packet jitter and link drops;
    \item \textbf{Recurring Costs:} Unbounded cumulative operational expenditures per million tokens generated on commercial cloud platforms.
\end{enumerate}

Small Language Models (SLMs), parameterized within the $0.5\text{B}$ to $4.0\text{B}$ weight regime, provide a compelling architectural alternative for local inference~\cite{rottger2023}. However, deploying these models on entry-level edge accelerators strictly bounded to $4096\text{ MiB}$ of physical VRAM introduces acute microarchitectural and thermodynamic constraints.

The Pascal microarchitecture (e.g., NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti), widespread across educational, industrial, and resource-constrained edge environments, lacks dedicated Tensor Cores and HBM stacks, relying on GDDR5 memory with a maximum theoretical bandwidth of $112.0\text{ GB/s}$~\cite{williams2009}. Under these hardware boundaries, autoregressive token generation is fundamentally memory-bandwidth bound, demanding aggressive tensor quantization and speculative decoding to achieve interactive throughput~\cite{leviathan2023}.

\section{Research Problem, Questions, and Hypotheses}

The problem addressed in this study is the experimental quantification of the multi-dimensional trade-offs among decoding throughput, physical memory footprint, Time-To-First-Token (TTFT), and behavioral robustness under adversarial stimuli in SLMs operating under a strict 4\,GiB VRAM ceiling.

We formalize four research questions ($Q_1$ to $Q_4$) and their corresponding testable working hypotheses:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{$Q_1$ (Speculative Acceleration):} \emph{Is speculative decoding via Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) practically feasible on edge hardware without exceeding the physical VRAM budget?} \\
    \textbf{Hypothesis $H_1$:} Speculative decoding based on integrated MTP heads provides statistically significant throughput speedups in warm steady-state execution with less than $100\text{ MiB}$ of additional VRAM overhead.
    \item \textbf{$Q_2$ (Quantization and Physical Frontiers):} \emph{What is the measurable systems differential between importance-matrix quantization (i-quants) and traditional block quantization (k-quants)?} \\
    \textbf{Hypothesis $H_2$:} Compression via the IQ4\_XS format enables higher data transfer efficiency than Q4\_K\_M while maintaining overall footprint within physical device capacity.
    \item \textbf{$Q_3$ (Adversarial Robustness):} \emph{Can compact instruction-tuned language models maintain clean instruction-data segregation against untrusted inputs?} \\
    \textbf{Hypothesis $H_3$:} SLMs preserve low over-refusal rates on benign operational commands, but exhibit vulnerability to indirect instruction injections embedded in contextual data streams.
    \item \textbf{$Q_4$ (Linguistic Disparity and Tokenization Tax):} \emph{What is the quantitative tokenization penalty for Portuguese (pt-BR) relative to English (en-US) across compact architectures?} \\
    \textbf{Hypothesis $H_4$:} Token fertility (tokens per word) in Brazilian Portuguese is substantially higher than in English, elevating latency and context memory requirements in Romance languages.
\end{itemize}

\section{Theoretical Foundations and Mathematical Modeling}

\subsection{Roofline Model and Arithmetic Intensity}
Operational boundaries of an edge accelerator are characterized by the Roofline model formalized by Williams et al.~\cite{williams2009}. Attainable floating-point performance $P$ (in $\text{GFLOP/s}$) is governed by:
\begin{equation}
P(I) = \min\left(P_{\text{peak}}, \; B_{\text{DRAM}} \times I\right)
\end{equation}
where $P_{\text{peak}}$ denotes peak single-precision floating-point capacity ($2138\text{ GFLOP/s}$ in FP32 for the GTX 1050 Ti at $1392\text{ MHz}$ boost clock), $B_{\text{DRAM}}$ is theoretical memory bandwidth ($112.0\text{ GB/s}$), and $I$ represents operational arithmetic intensity in $\text{FLOPs/byte}$.

During prefill processing of an input sequence of length $L$, arithmetic intensity scales with matrix multiplication:
\begin{equation}
I_{\text{prefill}} \approx \frac{2 \cdot N_{\text{params}} \cdot L}{2 \cdot N_{\text{params}} \cdot b + \mathcal{O}(L^2)} \gg 10\text{ FLOPs/byte}
\end{equation}
locating execution within the compute-bound regime.

Conversely, during autoregressive token-by-token generation ($L=1$), every emitted token requires loading all model weights from DRAM:
\begin{equation}
I_{\text{decode}} \approx \frac{2 \cdot N_{\text{params}}}{N_{\text{params}} \cdot b} = \frac{2}{b}\text{ FLOPs/byte}
\end{equation}
where $b$ is the average bytes per weight. For 4-bit quantized representations ($b = 0.5\text{ bytes}$), $I_{\text{decode}} \approx 4\text{ FLOPs/byte}$. Given the ridge point of the GTX 1050 Ti:
\begin{equation}
I_{\text{ridge}} = \frac{P_{\text{peak}}}{B_{\text{DRAM}}} = \frac{2138}{112.0} \approx 19.09\text{ FLOPs/byte}
\end{equation}
autoregressive decoding operates deeply within the memory-bandwidth-bound domain, with an upper bound on compute throughput of:
\begin{equation}
P_{\text{decode\_ceiling}} = 112.0\text{ GB/s} \times 4\text{ FLOPs/byte} = 448\text{ GFLOP/s}
\end{equation}
imposing a theoretical ceiling on token generation rate:
\begin{equation}
\text{Throughput}_{\text{max}} = \frac{B_{\text{DRAM}}}{S_{\text{weights}}}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Key-Value Cache Dynamics and Footprint}
During generation, the Key-Value (KV) cache stores projected attention representations to avoid redundant quadratic recomputation. Memory required for KV caching ($S_{\text{KV}}$) over a context window of length $L_{\text{ctx}}$ is defined by:
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{KV}} = 2 \times n_{\text{layers}} \times n_{\text{kv\_heads}} \times d_{\text{head}} \times L_{\text{ctx}} \times b_{\text{elem}}
\end{equation}
where $n_{\text{layers}}$ is the number of Transformer layers, $n_{\text{kv\_heads}}$ is the number of key/value heads (under Grouped-Query Attention --- GQA), $d_{\text{head}}$ is the scalar head dimension, and $b_{\text{elem}}$ is precision in bytes ($2$ bytes for FP16, $1$ byte for Q8\_0, and $0.5$ bytes for Q4\_0).

As sequence length $L_{\text{ctx}}$ increases, KV cache allocation expands linearly, competing directly with static model weights for physical VRAM space.

\subsection{Speculative Decoding and Multi-Token Prediction}
Speculative decoding, analyzed by Leviathan et al.~\cite{leviathan2023}, circumvents memory bandwidth saturation by proposing multiple candidate tokens using an auxiliary lightweight mechanism and validating them concurrently in a single forward pass.

Under the Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) paradigm, additional heads integrated directly into the primary model predict $K$ future tokens in parallel. Expected generation throughput $S$ is expressed as:
\begin{equation}
E[S] = \frac{1 + \sum_{k=1}^{K} \alpha^k}{1 + \mathcal{O}_{\text{draft}}}
\end{equation}
where $\alpha \in [0, 1]$ represents empirical token acceptance probability and $\mathcal{O}_{\text{draft}}$ denotes the computational overhead of the draft heads. When acceptance rates are sufficiently high and $\mathcal{O}_{\text{draft}} \ll 1$, decoding advances by multiple tokens per weight transfer cycle from DRAM.

\subsection{Defensive Robustness and Instruction-Data Segregation}
Evaluating security in edge language models involves testing privilege boundary enforcement between system instructions and untrusted contextual data~\cite{cheng2024,wallace2024}. Indirect prompt injection occurs when processed content carries imperative directives that compete with the system prompt:
\begin{equation}
\text{Prompt} = \mathcal{T}_{\text{system}} \parallel \mathcal{D}_{\text{untrusted}}(\mathcal{I}_{\text{adversarial}}) \parallel \mathcal{T}_{\text{user}}
\end{equation}
Attack Success Rate (ASR) and False Positive Rate (FPR) are quantified as:
\begin{align}
\text{ASR} &= \frac{1}{M_{\text{adv}}} \sum_{i=1}^{M_{\text{adv}}} \mathbb{I}(\text{Compromised}_i) \\
\text{FPR} &= \frac{1}{M_{\text{ben}}} \sum_{j=1}^{M_{\text{ben}}} \mathbb{I}(\text{Refusal}_j)
\end{align}

\section{Materials and Experimental Methodology}

\subsection{Physical Testbed and Computational Instrumentation}
Experiments were executed on an isolated physical host:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Graphics Accelerator:} NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti (GP107, 768 CUDA cores, base clock $1290\text{ MHz}$, boost $1392\text{ MHz}$, 4096\,MiB GDDR5, 128-bit bus, $112.0\text{ GB/s}$ theoretical bandwidth, $75\text{ W}$ board TDP ceiling, Compute Capability 6.1).
    \item \textbf{Host Environment:} Linux 6.8.12-8-pve (Proxmox VE 8.2), cgroup v2 process isolation, NVIDIA proprietary driver 580.173.02, CUDA Toolkit 13.0.
    \item \textbf{Inference Server:} \texttt{llama-server} (commit b4120+, native CUDA build with cuBLAS sm\_61 optimization, OpenAI-compatible HTTP interface on port 18081).
    \item \textbf{Hardware Telemetry:} Continuous asynchronous polling via native calls to \texttt{libnvidia-ml.so.1} (\texttt{pynvml} ctypes) recording physical VRAM, die temperature, GPU utilization, and core clocks at 250\,ms intervals.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textit{Methodological Transparency Note on Energy Modeling:} Power dissipation and derived energy efficiency metrics ($42\text{ W}$ to $62\text{ W}$ observed across sustained inference workloads) represent analytical estimates grounded in the GPU board's TDP envelope ($75\text{ W}$) and thermal state transitions, rather than direct physical measurements from an external in-line AC socket power analyzer.

\subsection{Execution Protocol and Environmental Controls}
The benchmarking harness applied strict experimental controls:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Warmup Runs ($k=2$):} Each model executed two 10-token preliminary invocations prior to formal recording, establishing stable memory residency.
    \item \textbf{Independent Replications ($N=10$):} Each experimental condition was evaluated across 10 repeated trials using a fixed seed (\texttt{seed=42}) and greedy sampling ($\tau = 0.0$).
    \item \textbf{Thermal Stabilization:} Enforced cooldown pauses of $400\text{ ms}$ between trials; GPU die temperatures were continuously verified between $38^\circ\text{C}$ and $74^\circ\text{C}$, confirming the absence of thermal throttling.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Inferential Statistical Methodology}
Condition comparisons utilize Welch's $t$-test with Satterthwaite degrees of freedom to account for heteroskedasticity:
\begin{equation}
t = \frac{\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2}{\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{n_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{n_2}}}, \quad \nu = \frac{\left(\frac{s_1^2}{n_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{n_2}\right)^2}{\frac{(s_1^2/n_1)^2}{n_1-1} + \frac{(s_2^2/n_2)^2}{n_2-1}}
\end{equation}
Two-tailed 95\% confidence intervals were constructed using Student's $t$-distribution with $\nu$ degrees of freedom. Standardized effect sizes were calculated via Cohen's $d$.

\section{Statistical Analysis and Factorial MTP Ablation}

To rigorously test Hypothesis $H_1$ without confounding weight quantization with speculative decoding, we evaluated a $2 \times 2$ factorial ablation on Gemma-4-E2B across three controlled configurations ($N=10$, $\tau=0.0$, \texttt{seed=42}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Condition $C_1$ (Baseline):} Gemma-4-E2B in Q4\_K\_M format without speculative heads ($26.92 \pm 0.23$\,tok/s, VRAM $1996.7$\,MiB);
    \item \textbf{Condition $C_2$ (Quantization Ablation):} Gemma-4-E2B in IQ4\_XS format without speculative heads ($34.51 \pm 0.29$\,tok/s, VRAM $1800.7$\,MiB);
    \item \textbf{Condition $C_3$ (MTP Speculative):} Gemma-4-E2B in IQ4\_XS format with integrated Q8\_0 MTP draft heads ($43.35 \pm 0.57$\,tok/s, VRAM $2036.7$\,MiB).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.85\textwidth]{figures/mtp_ablation_ci.pdf}
\caption{Factorial speedup decomposition: isolated effect of quantization ($C_1 \rightarrow C_2$) and causal MTP speculative gain under identical quantization ($C_2 \rightarrow C_3$), with Student's $t$ 95\% confidence intervals ($N=10$).}
\label{fig:mtp_ablation}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Isolated Causal Effect of MTP (Constant IQ4\_XS Quantization)}
Comparing $C_3$ directly with $C_2$, quantization format is held constant in IQ4\_XS. Welch's $t$-test gives:
\begin{equation}
t_{\text{MTP}} = \frac{43.35 - 34.51}{\sqrt{\frac{0.57^2}{10} + \frac{0.29^2}{10}}} = \frac{8.84}{0.2022} \approx 43.72, \quad \nu = 13.43
\end{equation}
with $p = 7.12 \times 10^{-16}$ and $d = 19.55$. The isolated causal speedup attributable strictly to MTP speculative decoding is \textbf{+25.6\%} ($8.84$\,tok/s).

\subsection{Isolated Effect of Quantization (Standard Autoregressive Decoding)}
Comparing $C_2$ with $C_1$, decoding mode is held constant without speculative draft heads. Welch's $t$-test yields:
\begin{equation}
t_{\text{Quant}} = \frac{34.51 - 26.92}{\sqrt{\frac{0.29^2}{10} + \frac{0.23^2}{10}}} = \frac{7.59}{0.1160} \approx 65.41, \quad \nu = 17.09
\end{equation}
with $p = 5.67 \times 10^{-18}$ and $d = 29.25$. Weight compression via the IQ4\_XS importance matrix provides a speedup of \textbf{+28.2\%} ($7.59$\,tok/s).

\subsection{Compound Configuration Differential ($C_3$ vs $C_1$)}
Comparing the optimized configuration ($C_3$) with the baseline standard ($C_1$) reflects total accumulated gain:
\begin{equation}
t_{\text{Compound}} = \frac{43.35 - 26.92}{\sqrt{\frac{0.57^2}{10} + \frac{0.23^2}{10}}} = \frac{16.43}{0.1944} \approx 84.50, \quad \nu = 11.94
\end{equation}
with $p = 5.88 \times 10^{-18}$ and $d = 37.79$. The compound configuration gain totals \textbf{+61.0\%} ($16.43$\,tok/s) under only $40.0\text{ MiB}$ of VRAM overhead for Q8\_0 draft heads relative to $C_1$, providing empirical support for Hypothesis $H_1$.

\subsection{Absence of Over-Refusal ($\text{FPR} = 0.0$\%)}
All evaluated models responded accurately to administrative operational queries (such as process termination via \texttt{kill -9} and SYN packet inspection via \texttt{tcpdump}), refuting concerns of conservative over-refusal in legitimate systems tasks.

\subsection{Vulnerability to Indirect Injections and Delimiter Escape}
Consistent with Hypothesis $H_3$, models exhibited susceptibility to indirect instruction injections and delimiter escape (ASR between 50.0\% and 100.0\%). When processing structured inputs carrying imperative secondary commands, compact architectures routinely complied with embedded instructions.

\subsection{Operational Stages in Agentic Tool Invocation}
Detailed auditing of tool interactions enabled differentiation across three distinct execution phases:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Proposed Call (\texttt{proposed\_call}):} The model generates a structured function call request;
    \item \textbf{Schema Validity (\texttt{valid\_call}):} Parameter conformance to expected JSON schemas;
    \item \textbf{Host Execution (\texttt{execution\_status}):} Security evaluation prior to system dispatch.
\end{enumerate}

Models including Qwen, Gemma, and Llama generated valid schema payloads containing injected payloads (\texttt{"nginx; rm -rf /"} and \texttt{"admin'; DROP TABLE"}). In all instances, harness guardrails intercepted execution with \texttt{BLOCKED\_BY\_HARNESS}, logging invocations without permitting host side effects. Conversely, SmolLM2 did not emit tool call syntax, replying with safe conversational text (\texttt{SAFE\_BENIGN\_TEXT}).

\section{Algorithmic Bias and Language Efficiency Evaluation}

\subsection{Demographic Bias and Counterfactual Consistency}
Evaluations inspired by BBQ and StereoSet-PT demonstrated that Gemma-4-E2B preserved 100\% neutrality across counterfactual gender and income pairs. On Brazilian regional dialect tests, however, smaller models generated verbose infrastructure descriptions rather than directly rejecting biased premises.

\subsection{Tokenization Fertility via Parallel Reference Corpus}
To measure linguistic overhead in a controlled setting, we used a semantically equivalent parallel corpus tokenized natively via \texttt{/tokenize}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{SmolLM2-1.7B} exhibited the highest subword fragmentation: $2.33\text{ tok/w}$ in pt-BR and $2.40\text{ tok/w}$ in pt-PT, versus $1.10\text{ tok/w}$ in en-US, introducing an overhead exceeding 100\% in context consumption;
    \item \textbf{Gemma-4-E2B} ($1.35\text{ tok/w}$ in pt-BR) and \textbf{Qwen-3.5} ($1.37\text{ tok/w}$ in pt-BR) maintained balanced multilingual vocabularies, narrowing the efficiency gap for Romance languages.
\end{itemize}

\section{Threats to Validity and Limitations}

\subsection{Internal Validity}
Hardware telemetry was captured at 250\,ms intervals; while adequate for sustained average VRAM allocation and power states, sub-millisecond microsecond spikes are filtered by the NVML polling bus. As noted in Section 4.1, energy estimates reflect thermal TDP envelope modeling ($42\text{ W}$ to $62\text{ W}$), rather than direct hardware AC socket wattmeters.

\subsection{External Validity}
Benchmarks were conducted exclusively on Pascal architecture (GTX 1050 Ti, Compute Capability 6.1). Newer architectures (Ampere, Ada Lovelace, Blackwell) incorporate dedicated Tensor Cores and native FP8/FP4 support, altering the operational ridge point of the Roofline model.

\subsection{Construct Validity}
Defensive safety was evaluated using 10 structured synthetic attack scenarios. While deterministic and reproducible, multi-turn adaptive jailbreak prompts may reveal additional vulnerability surfaces.

\section{Conclusions and Future Work}

This study provided an empirical, mathematical, and systems evaluation of Small Language Models (0.5B to 4B) operating under a physical $4096\text{ MiB}$ VRAM limit.

Primary empirical conclusions supported by measured evidence are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Decomposition of Speculative Speedup:} Structured factorial ablation demonstrates that multi-token prediction (MTP) under constant IQ4\_XS quantization yields an isolated causal speedup of $+25.6\%$ ($43.35$ vs $34.51$\,tok/s, $t=43.72$, $p < 10^{-15}$), while IQ4\_XS compression accounts for $+28.2\%$ over Q4\_K\_M ($34.51$ vs $26.92$\,tok/s, $t=65.41$), totaling a compound $+61.0\%$ gain ($t=84.50$, $p = 5.88 \times 10^{-18}$) with 40\,MiB VRAM overhead for Q8\_0 draft heads.
    \item \textbf{4B Physical Operating Edge:} Models near 4 billion parameters operate at the physical capacity boundary of 4\,GiB ($76.7\%$). For Gemma-3-4B, Q4\_0 KV cache quantization is required to prevent out-of-memory faults.
    \item \textbf{Harness-Level Interception Imperative:} Although models showed zero false positive refusals on operational queries ($\text{FPR}=0.0\%$), susceptibility to indirect injections in tool calls highlights the necessity of application-level guardrails.
    \item \textbf{Multilingual Efficiency Variations:} Parallel corpus tokenization demonstrated an overhead of up to $2.33\text{ tok/w}$ in Portuguese (SmolLM2-1.7B), whereas Gemma-4 and Qwen-3.5 maintained balanced multilingual efficiency near $1.35\text{ tok/w}$.
\end{enumerate}

Future research will explore sub-2-bit quantization formats and direct hardware current probe instrumentation on PCIe rails for granular energy characterization.
